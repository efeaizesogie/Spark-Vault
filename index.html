How to use the vault

The brainstorm loop

The order the video works in, from a blank page to a finished design. Run it top to bottom for a new project, or jump to the step where you’re stuck.

  1. Collect references

    Before you prompt, find two or three things you love. Claude copies a reference far better than it follows a description.

    Savee · Visual Journal · 60fps

  2. Lock the brand file

    Model a great brand guideline and put your colours, type and rules into one file you can hand to any tool.

    Brand Guidelines · Rebrand Gallery · Logo System

  3. Pick type and icons

    Fonts are the fastest tell of AI slop. Choose a real typeface and a consistent icon set early.

    Typewolf · Fonts In Use · Fontshare · Flaticon

  4. Build it section by section

    Treat the navbar, hero, bento, CTA, footer and 404 as separate pieces. Find the best example of each and combine them.

    Navbar · CTA · Footer · 404s · Bento Grids

  5. Study real flows

    For apps, look at every screen of the best product in your category before you design your own.

    Mobbin · Refero

  6. Add motion last

    Hover lifts, soft presses, counters and loops make a design feel expensive. Hand Claude a clip to match.

    LottieFiles · Transitions · Motion Primitives

  7. Run the design loop

    Give Claude a gold-standard reference and let a critic agent compare its output against it until they match.

    Design Loop skill · Higgsfield

  8. Simplify and de-slop

    Check that the page passes a glance test, then strip the AI tells from the copy and tighten every sentence.

    Don’t Make Me Think · Slop Monster · STE100
